# Muhit Telecom — logo brief (round 1)

## Design brief

- **Brand:** MUHIT TELECOM. “Muhit Telecom GSM” and “Proprietor: Masum Hossain” stay in business/contact information rather than making the core logo too long.
- **Business:** Mobile phones, everyday gadgets and accessories, plus mobile and computer repair services in Hili, with online orders across Bangladesh.
- **Audience:** Local Hili and Hakimpur customers, and nationwide online shoppers who need a dependable seller and repair team.
- **Desired feel:** Premium, modern, trustworthy, helpful, and locally rooted.
- **Avoid:** Generic shield badges, literal phone silhouettes, Wi‑Fi bars, and busy circuit patterns.
- **Equity to keep:** MT initials from the existing mark; navy, cobalt, and sky blue remain palette assumptions for the next stage.
- **Must work on:** Website header and footer, favicon, social avatar, printed receipt, and store sign.
- **Success criteria:** A clear silhouette at 16 px, one-colour reproduction, balanced light/dark use, and enough flexibility for product sales and repair services.
- **Round 1 format:** Black-and-white first, before colour is chosen.

## Category research and word map

In the bundled reference library, ecommerce/retail marks often use containment and blue; hardware/device marks commonly use one or two colours, with simple monograms, abstract geometry, or pictorial symbols. The concepts avoid the most familiar phone, shield, and signal-bar shortcuts. The useful intersections for Muhit Telecom are **MT initials + dependable service**, **open forms + a point of contact**, and **a local gateway + access to everyday tech**.

## Concept longlist

1. **MT Junction:** Custom M and T letterforms sit together in a compact, cut-out square mark.
2. **Local Link:** Two inward-facing open brackets meet a central point, joining the shop, repair desk, and customer.
3. **Hili Gateway:** A sturdy arch with a crossbar evokes a welcoming local threshold for tech and repair help.
4. **Open Circuit:** A single clean route folds into the M’s centre without drawing a literal circuit board.
5. **Service Bridge:** Two horizontal paths meet through one upright, expressing product and repair support with shared geometry.
6. **Counterpoint:** A small centre point is framed by two open sides, making the customer connection the visual focus.
7. **MUHIT Wordmark:** A custom M gives the full name a distinctive start, with TELECOM as a quieter descriptor.
8. **Joined Stems:** The central diagonals of M resolve into a T crossbar, turning the initials into one continuous lettermark.
9. **Repair Loop:** A broken-square route closes through a single joining segment, suggesting careful repair and return.
10. **Local Portal:** A squared doorway with a central notch combines local approachability and modern precision.

**Recommendation:** A · MT Junction preserves the existing initials while making a compact avatar and favicon. B is the most abstract and flexible; C has the strongest local/place story but may feel more architectural.

**Assumptions:** The brand remains “Muhit Telecom”; this refresh may modernise its current MT mark while keeping the initials familiar. Navy/cobalt/sky are treated as starting colours only, so the first comparison is grayscale. No trademark clearance has been performed.
