# Muhit Telecom — gadget logo concepts (round 2)

## Direction update

The requested icon should clearly relate to gadgets. This round uses a familiar phone silhouette, a charger, and a device-care symbol so the options read as technology marks before colour is introduced.

## Brand brief

- **Brand:** MUHIT TELECOM. “GSM” and “Proprietor: Masum Hossain” remain supporting business details, not part of the core mark.
- **Business:** Mobile phones, everyday electronics and accessories, plus mobile and computer repair in Hili and online orders across Bangladesh.
- **Audience:** Hili/Hakimpur customers and nationwide online shoppers looking for compatible gadgets and reliable local service.
- **Desired feel:** Premium, modern, trustworthy, helpful, locally rooted.
- **Avoid:** Busy catalogue collages and generic shields, Wi‑Fi bars, or circuit-board patterns.
- **Keep:** Familiar MT initials as a useful brand link; navy, cobalt and sky blue are palette assumptions for the next phase.
- **Must work on:** Website header/footer, favicon, social avatar, receipt and store sign.
- **Success criteria:** Clear gadget association, distinct silhouette at 16 px, one-colour use, and room for both retail and repair.
- **Category research:** The bundled ecommerce/hardware reference sets commonly use blue, containment and one or two colours. Round 2 compares simple device-led silhouettes in grayscale, then checks light/dark, reversed and small-size use.

## Updated concept longlist

1. **Pocket MT:** A phone silhouette frames a custom MT monogram as a compact gadget-first mark.
2. **Charge Source:** A single adapter silhouette and port stand for compatible everyday electronics.
3. **Care Spark:** A device screen and one sharp spark imply looking after useful gadgets.
4. **Open Device:** Four device-frame corners hold a central M-shaped connection path.
5. **Repair Ready:** A screen outline and precision-tool stroke merge into one repair mark.
6. **Pocket Link:** The phone side buttons become two connection points beside a clear M.
7. **Everyday Tech:** A compact tablet silhouette frames a simple central connector, leaving the name to do the brand work.
8. **MT Port:** The T crossbar becomes a USB-C port inside an outlined device.
9. **Audio Companion:** A minimal earbud case shape uses the opening lid as a gateway around MT.
10. **Device Care:** A rounded screen encloses a restrained four-point glint for tested, cared-for electronics.
